WHY ONE-TO-ONE?

Start with the student’s thinking.

Group teaching works well. When a student has not understood something, one-to-one tuition gives us time to uncover why and find an explanation that works for them.

01

What do they already understand?

Identify secure knowledge and the gaps beneath the current topic.

02

Where did the misconception begin?

Work back to the source of the difficulty.

03

Is the barrier actually mathematical?

Support the skills that make the science accessible.

04

Which explanation works for this student?

Adapt the teaching, rather than repeating the same explanation.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Tuition FAQs.

Is tuition online?+

Yes. Tuition is delivered online, making specialist support available to students across the UK.

Which examination boards are supported?+

Include your examination board in your enquiry. I will confirm that I can support your course before arranging tuition.

Is homework set?+

Targeted independent work and resources can support learning between sessions. The work is chosen around what the student needs.

How often should sessions take place?+

We can discuss the student’s goals and availability to agree an appropriate frequency.

Can tuition help with an upcoming examination?+

Tuition can identify gaps and focus practice ahead of an examination. Enquire with the subject, board and exam date so we can discuss what is realistic.

What happens if a lesson is missed?+

Policy to be confirmed. Cancellation and missed-session arrangements will be agreed before booking.

How does payment work?+

Payment arrangements to be confirmed before booking.
