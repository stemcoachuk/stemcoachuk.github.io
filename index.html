About Paul

Paul is an experienced secondary science teacher with eight years of experience as a Head of Science and senior school leader. He currently teaches Physics and Chemistry across secondary and post-16 education.

His wider professional experience includes external examining, curriculum and assessment development, teacher development and digital strategy. He is also undertaking doctoral research in Nuclear Astrophysics.

Remaining an active classroom teacher is fundamental to The STEM Coach approach. Whether working with a school, teacher or individual student, his support remains closely connected to the realities of teaching, learning and assessment.
