YOUR QUESTIONS

Review FAQs.

What should we prepare?+

We agree the focus and relevant documentation in advance. This can include curriculum plans and assessment examples. The precise preparation is tailored to the department.

Will the review cover all three sciences?+

The scope can explore coherence across Biology, Chemistry and Physics, alongside progression between key stages.

Will we receive a report?+

Yes. Verbal feedback is followed by a concise developmental report containing clear priorities and recommendations.

Is this an inspection?+

No. The review is collaborative and developmental, designed to support practical improvement.

How do we arrange a date?+

Send a school enquiry with your preferred timeframe. We can discuss a suitable date and scope.

What does all inclusive cover?+

The stated price is £595 for the full-day review, preparation and developmental report. Specific visit arrangements will be confirmed before booking.
